Metadata Removal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy covers Metadata Removal, the free service at validprivacy.github.io that removes hidden data from photos, videos, and audio files.

Information We Collect

Metadata Removal does not collect any information. Your files are never uploaded. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

How Your Files Are Handled

  • The files you choose are read and cleaned entirely inside your own browser, on your own device
  • The page tells your browser to block every network connection, so the page cannot send your files or anything about them anywhere
  • The clean copies exist only in your browser's memory until you download them or save them to a folder you choose
  • Your original files are never changed
  • Closing or reloading the page discards everything

Hosting

The page itself is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives standard web request details, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the page. That is covered by GitHub's Privacy Statement. Your files are never part of those requests.

Sharing

Metadata Removal shares nothing with anyone, because it never receives anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to byrook@proton.me.
