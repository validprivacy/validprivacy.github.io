Valid Vault Enterprise

Fully offline password management for organizations that keep credentials off the cloud: hospitals, law firms, banks, defense contractors, utilities, and government offices.

How to use it

  1. Set the funding. Drag the Funding slider to the amount you are considering, from $2,000 to $5,000,000, or type an exact amount in the plan.
  2. Set the customer mix. Choose how many small offices, mid-size organizations, and large regulated organizations sign in year 1, and how fast new signings grow each year.
  3. Adjust the plan. Change pricing, salaries, hiring, and costs to test other assumptions.
  4. Read the results. See how long the funding lasts, when the company turns a profit, annual recurring revenue for each year, and the investor's ownership and return at year 5.

Who buys an offline password manager

  • Defense contractors and suppliers handling controlled information
  • Hospitals, clinics, and other healthcare providers
  • Law firms and accounting firms holding client records
  • Banks and credit unions
  • Utilities and other critical infrastructure
  • Government offices and air-gapped networks

Comparison

Bitwarden, a cloud password manager founded in 2015, raised an undisclosed Series A in 2019 and a $100 million growth round in 2022 led by PSG with Battery Ventures (TechCrunch). Bitwarden lists Teams at $4 and Enterprise at $6 per user per month (costbench).

How it works: new customers sign evenly through each year, pay the setup fee once, then pay license fees every month. A share cancel each year. Sales and support work is counted in mid-size customers: a large customer counts as 3, a small one as a quarter. The founder covers the first share of sales work each year and salespeople are hired for the rest. Support staff scale with customers, engineers are added each year, and employer costs (payroll taxes, benefits) are added on top of salaries. Capital to reach profit is the deepest point cash reaches before revenue overtakes costs. Investor return uses the company value at year 5 (ARR times the multiple) and the investor's ownership, before any later rounds. The customer mix, seat counts, salaries, the valuation cap, and the multiple are assumptions to set with real data and investor terms. Founder salary reference: the median for software developers is $135,980 (BLS, May 2025).
