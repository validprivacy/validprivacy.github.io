About Valid Privacy

Valid Privacy builds software that runs on the devices people already own and keeps their data there.

The company was founded by Michael Carter, a software engineer working in Rust and JavaScript, with a background as a Radioman and in IT and network support for hospital and campus environments, including cryptographic key handling, transmission security, and protecting sensitive records on shared infrastructure.

Structure

The products and the network are deliberately held by separate organizations.

Valid Privacy

The company. It builds and supports Valid Vault and Valid Symphony, and it is where commercial work, partnerships, and support happen.

The Valid Foundation

A non-profit being organized to steward Valid Network independently, with its own board, so the network does not depend on any one company.
